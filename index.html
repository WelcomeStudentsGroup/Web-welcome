What we do

Three ways we accompany your journey

We guide your path in Australia. With 10 years in international education — and having lived the international-student experience ourselves — we understand the challenges and the opportunities.

Study

Free educational advisory and representation of premier institutions across English, vocational (VET) and higher education programs.

WelcomeHub — Community

A space to learn, connect and grow professionally: migration seminars, education fairs and networking events built for our students.

Migration

We work with experienced lawyers and registered migration agents who help you choose the migration pathway that fits your goals.

How we help

Your journey abroad, in six steps

  1. 01

    Schedule your consultation

    A free, no-obligation call with one of our advisers to understand your goals.

  2. 02

    Choose your city

    From Brisbane's student life to Melbourne's art scene — we help you match a city to your goals.

  3. 03

    Select your course & institution

    English, VET or higher education — matched to your objectives and budget.

  4. 04

    Apply for your visa

    Guidance through the visa application with our migration partners.

  5. 05

    Prepare your trip

    Airport pickup, accommodation guide, phone plan and bank account support.

  6. 06

    Prepare your CV

    CV tips and guidance to apply for your work permit once you arrive.
